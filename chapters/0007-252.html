This eight-chapter volume is an original condensed retelling based on Wu Cheng'en's public-domain classic, not the complete original text or a line-by-line translation.

The closer the pilgrims came to Spirit Mountain, the more their trials resembled ordinary life. On Bramble Ridge no demon king blocked them; endless thorn vines simply covered the road overnight. Wukong cut a path, Bajie uprooted the thickest growth, Wujing cleared the branches, and Tripitaka dismounted to carry stones. For once they did not wait for a single hero to defeat an enemy. Each did a small part, and slowly they moved forward.

At the Little Thunderclap Monastery, sacred light filled the hall and a figure calling himself the Buddha sat upon a lotus throne. Tripitaka bowed at once, but Wukong smelled the Yellow-Brow Demon's aura. Golden cymbals closed around the monkey, and a magical sack swallowed every divine helper he summoned. Only when Maitreya arrived and laid a trap in a melon field was the runaway attendant captured and the stolen treasures recovered.

On Lion-Camel Ridge, the Green Lion, White Elephant, and Great Peng ruled a city of monsters. The Great Peng could match Wukong's speed, while the Bottle of Primordial Forces nearly dissolved the monkey into blood. When Wukong finally escaped, he understood that some evils could not be overcome by solitary courage. He sought the aid of Manjusri, Samantabhadra, and the Buddha, and the three monsters were returned to their proper masters.

On the final night, the travelers slept beneath pine trees. Bajie asked how much the scriptures weighed. Wujing guessed they were lighter than the luggage. Wukong said they might be heavier than Five Elements Mountain.

“If they rest only on the shoulders, they have a weight,” Tripitaka said. “If they enter our conduct, their weight can no longer be seen.”

Wukong lay in the branches and watched the stars. Once he had believed freedom meant that no one could command him. Now he understood that freedom could also mean choosing a duty—and carrying it to the end.

At dawn, golden light rose in the distance. Spirit Mountain appeared above the sea of clouds.
